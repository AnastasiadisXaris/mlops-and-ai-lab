# Papers Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This folder contains academic papers, literature reviews, research summaries, critical analyses, annotations, publication drafts, methodological notes, citations, and scientific resources related to AI, Machine Learning, recommendation systems, marketing analytics, LLMs, MLOps, and doctoral research.

The goal is to build a structured academic research ecosystem for literature reviews, systematic reviews, scoping reviews, thesis development, publication preparation, and research synthesis.


---

## Review Types

### Systematic Reviews

Structured evidence synthesis using predefined methodology.

**Characteristics:** reproducible · transparent · protocol-driven · PRISMA-based

**Key components:** search strategy · inclusion criteria · screening process · synthesis

### Scoping Reviews

Broad mapping of research domains — useful for emerging fields, interdisciplinary topics, and conceptual mapping.

**Example topics:** AI in education · LLM personalization · conjoint analysis + ML

### Literature Reviews

Narrative or conceptual synthesis focused on theoretical integration, conceptual analysis, and framework development.

## Best Practices

- annotate while reading
- synthesize across papers, not just within them
- track limitations and open questions
- connect theory with implementation
- organize references early
- document search strategies for reproducibility
