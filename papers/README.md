# Papers Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This folder contains academic papers, literature reviews, research summaries, critical analyses, annotations, publication drafts, methodological notes, citations, and scientific resources related to AI, Machine Learning, recommendation systems, marketing analytics, LLMs, MLOps, and doctoral research.

The goal is to build a structured academic research ecosystem for:

- literature reviews
- systematic reviews
- scoping reviews
- thesis development
- publication preparation
- research synthesis
- scientific experimentation
- conceptual mapping

---

# Research Philosophy

Research is not merely:

```text
collecting PDFs
```

It is:

```text
building structured understanding
through evidence, critique, synthesis,
and conceptual integration
```
